# Nexus Chat Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Nexus Chat application may collect, use, store, and protect. By using Nexus Chat, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the application.

## 1. General Information

Nexus Chat is a messaging application for exchanging messages, creating group chats, making audio and video calls, sending media files, and communicating with other users.

To provide these features, we may process data required for registration, authentication, message delivery, calls, security, user support, and service improvement.

## 2. Data We May Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate Nexus Chat and provide its features.

### 2.1. Account Data

When you register or use the application, we may process:

- username;
- phone number or email address, if used for login;
- profile photo;
- user identifier;
- account settings;
- account creation date;
- activity status, if this feature is available in the application.

### 2.2. Messages and Chat Data

To operate the messaging service, the application may process:

- text messages;
- messages in private and group chats;
- information about chat participants;
- date and time of message sending;
- message delivery and read statuses;
- attachments sent by users.

Message content is used to deliver messages to recipients and display chat history to users.

### 2.3. Media Files and Attachments

If a user sends or uploads files, the application may process:

- images;
- videos;
- audio files;
- documents;
- voice messages;
- other attachments sent by the user.

Media files are processed only to provide file sharing, display attachments, and deliver them to other users.

### 2.4. Call Data

To provide audio and video calls, Nexus Chat may process technical data required to establish and maintain a connection:

- call participant identifiers;
- call start and end time;
- technical connection parameters;
- connection quality information;
- IP addresses and network data required to establish the connection.

If the application uses direct connections or third-party call technologies, some technical data may be transmitted to communication infrastructure providers only to the extent necessary to operate the call.

### 2.5. Contacts

If a user grants access to contacts, the application may use them to help find known users and display contacts in the application.

Access to contacts is requested only with the user's permission. The user may deny access or disable it in the device settings.

### 2.6. Device Data and Technical Information

For stability, security, and error diagnostics, we may process:

- device type;
- operating system and version;
- application version;
- device language;
- IP address;
- crash and error information;
- technical application logs;
- device or app installation identifiers, if required for service operation.

### 2.7. Push Notifications

To send notifications, the application may use device push tokens. A push token is required to deliver notifications about new messages, calls, and other application events.

Users may disable notifications in the device settings.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data for the following purposes:

- creating and maintaining user accounts;
- authentication and account protection;
- delivering messages and attachments;
- operating private and group chats;
- providing audio and video calls;
- sending push notifications;
- displaying user profiles;
- ensuring application security;
- preventing abuse, spam, and fraud;
- diagnosing errors and improving application stability;
- handling support requests;
- complying with legal obligations, where applicable.

We do not sell users' personal data to third parties.

## 4. User-Generated Content

Nexus Chat is a service where users may create and send their own content, including messages, images, videos, files, and other materials.

Users are responsible for the content they send through the application. Users must not send illegal, harmful, offensive, rights-infringing, or otherwise prohibited content.

We may take action regarding content or accounts if we receive a complaint, detect a violation of service rules, or are required to do so by law.

## 5. Sharing Data With Third Parties

We may share data with third parties only when necessary for the operation of the application or when required by law.

Such recipients may include:

- cloud infrastructure providers;
- push notification services;
- analytics and crash diagnostics providers;
- communication service providers required for audio and video calls;
- payment or other service providers, if such features are added;
- government authorities, where required by law.

Third parties receive only the data necessary to perform the relevant function.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to:

- provide application features;
- maintain the user's account;
- comply with legal obligations;
- resolve disputes;
- ensure service security;
- prevent fraud and abuse.

Users may request deletion of their account and related data, where such deletion is legally and technically applicable.

Some data may be retained for a limited period after account deletion if required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or backup purposes.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Such measures may include:

- access control for systems;
- encrypted connections;
- server infrastructure protection;
- monitoring of suspicious activity;
- limiting data access to authorized personnel only.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to:

- obtain information about the data being processed;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- request deletion of an account or data;
- restrict data processing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- disable the application's access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, notifications, and other permissions through device settings.

To exercise these rights, users may contact us using the contact details provided in the "Contact Us" section.

## 9. App Permissions

Nexus Chat may request the following device permissions:

- camera — for photos, videos, and video calls;
- microphone — for voice messages and calls;
- photos and media library — for sending and saving images and videos;
- contacts — for finding known users, if the user grants permission;
- notifications — for receiving notifications about messages and calls;
- files — for sending documents and attachments.

Granting permissions is voluntary. Some application features may not be available without the relevant permission.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Nexus Chat is not intended for use by children below the age permitted by applicable law and App Store rules.

If we become aware that we have processed a child's personal data without the required consent of a parent or legal guardian, we will take reasonable steps to delete such data.

User data may be processed and stored on servers located in different countries. In such cases, we take measures to protect the data in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable legal requirements.
